PAX TV Hospitality is an intelligent, cloud-managed interactive TV launcher designed specifically for luxury hotels, resorts, serviced apartments, and hospitality venues.

Transform ordinary in-room guest televisions into a personalized digital concierge, high-definition entertainment hub, and interactive service portal.

KEY FEATURES AND GUEST SERVICES

1. Personalized Guest Welcome & Hotel Home Screen
- Displays dynamic guest greetings, localized time, live weather, and room identifiers.
- Showcase hotel amenities, restaurant recommendations, and daily events directly upon TV startup.
- Cloud-managed themes with seamless brand customization.

2. In-Room Guest Services & Digital Concierge
- Interactive hotel facility showcase (Spa, Pool, Gym, Dining, Room Service, City Tours).
- Digital food & beverage menus, concierge request shortcuts, and front desk assistance.
- Multi-language navigation support for international travelers.

3. Live TV & Premium Streaming Integration
- High-definition Live TV channels with fast remote control channel surfing.
- Easy access to popular streaming and entertainment apps (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube, Disney+ Hotstar, ZEE5, Sony LIV).
- Automatic privacy management ensuring guest credentials and viewing history are wiped upon check-out.

4. Wireless Screen Casting
- Built-in wireless mobile screen mirroring allowing guests to cast their favorite movies, music, and photos directly from Android and iOS smartphones.

5. Cloud CMS & Hotel Fleet Management
- Centralized hotel administrator dashboard to push firmware updates, announcements, and advertisements in real-time.
- Quick setup and pairing via on-screen QR Code or remote pairing code.
- Remote device health monitoring and diagnostic telemetry.
